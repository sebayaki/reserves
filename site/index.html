The details behind the data

A note on
what we count.

Publicly reported holdings. Clearly stated limits.

01

Holdings, not ownership estimates.

Government balances come from identified public reports. Seized assets in custody may be included and are described at country level. Private citizens, exchange customer deposits and estimates of national adoption are excluded.

02

Businesses, by headquarters.

The switch adds selected companies with disclosed treasury balances, assigned to their headquarters country. It does not cover every company. These assets belong to the companies, not the government. ETF and custodian client holdings are excluded.

03

Two different clocks.

Holdings are curated disclosure snapshots, with dates on every source. USD valuations apply one price snapshot to these balances. They are indicative valuations, not synchronized live balances. Prices are a dated snapshot, refreshed separately from holdings.

04

Gaps stay visible.

A dash means an amount is not verified in this dataset. Totals sum only known amounts. Country ranks and percentages compare the selected dataset, not all global holdings. Including businesses can create partial country totals.
